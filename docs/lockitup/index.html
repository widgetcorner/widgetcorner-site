Lock It Up

Keep your Spades game moving. Track bids, books, and bags while Lock It Up handles the math.

About

Lock It Up is a free, ad-free scorekeeper for four-player partnership Spades at a real table. Add four players, choose partners and house rules, and let the app calculate each round’s score.

Scoring works offline on your phone or tablet, so you can keep your attention on the cards and the table.

Features

  • Your table, your rules. Customize nil, blind nil, bag penalties, 10-for-200, and Boston. Play to a target score or a fixed number of hands, with an optional mercy rule.
  • Quick bids and books. Enter each team’s bid, mark individual nil or blind nil bids, and record the books won. Preview the scoring breakdown before adding the round.
  • The game at a glance. Follow scores, bids, bags, and the next dealer. Check the current game’s round history and score chart to see how the lead changed.
  • Easy corrections. Undo the last round, or record a points adjustment with a reason for a renege, misdeal, or another table ruling.
  • Pause, resume, rematch. Save unfinished games and pick them up later. Start a rematch with the same players and seats when you’re ready for another game.
  • History and stats. Review final scores, team win–loss records, win rates, and individual nil performance. Export completed-game history or delete completed stats from the app.
  • Your history on your devices. Sync completed-game history across your own iPhones and iPads with iCloud, or Android devices with Google Play Games. Apple and Android histories stay separate; unfinished games stay on their original device.

Privacy

No Widget Corner account. No ads or cross-app tracking. Your rules and unfinished games stay on your device. Optional completed-game sync uses your iCloud or Google Play Games account; those platform services may process account, analytics, and diagnostic data.
