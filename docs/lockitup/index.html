A closer look

Made for the people at your table.

Lock It Up is a free, ad-free scorekeeper for four-player partnership Spades at a real table. Add four players, choose partners and house rules, and let the app calculate each round’s score.

Scoring works offline on your phone or tablet, so you can keep your attention on the cards and the table.

The little details

A good hand. A better scorekeeper.

  • Your table, your rules

    Customize nil, blind nil, bag penalties, 10-for-200, and Boston. Play to a target score or a fixed number of hands, with an optional mercy rule.

  • Quick bids and books

    Enter each team’s bid, mark individual nil or blind nil bids, and record the books won. Preview the scoring breakdown before adding the round.

  • The game at a glance

    Follow scores, bids, bags, and the next dealer. Check the current game’s round history and score chart to see how the lead changed.

  • Easy corrections

    Undo the last round, or record a points adjustment with a reason for a renege, misdeal, or another table ruling.

  • Pause, resume, rematch

    Save unfinished games and pick them up later. Start a rematch with the same players and seats when you’re ready for another game.

  • History and stats

    Review final scores, team win–loss records, win rates, and individual nil performance. Export completed-game history or delete completed stats from the app.

  • Your history on your devices

    Sync completed-game history across your own iPhones and iPads with iCloud, or Android devices with Google Play Games. Apple and Android histories stay separate; unfinished games stay on their original device.

Privacy

Your time. Your device. Your business.

No Widget Corner account. No ads or cross-app tracking. Your rules and unfinished games stay on your device. Optional completed-game sync uses your iCloud or Google Play Games account; those platform services may process account, analytics, and diagnostic data.
