Lock It Up

A focused scorekeeper for four-player partnership Spades — bids, books, bags, house rules, history, and stats.

About

Lock It Up replaces pencil-and-paper scorekeeping without trying to replace the table. Enter four players, choose how your group plays, and the app handles round scoring, dealer rotation, and the race to 250, 300, or 500.

It is local-first, free, and has no ads. Games in progress stay on the device. Confirmed completed games can sync between devices on the same platform through iCloud or Google Play Games; iPhone and Android history do not sync with each other.

Features

  • Your house rules. Configure nil, blind nil, bag penalties, 10-for-200, Boston, minimum bids, target score, and high cards.
  • Fast round entry. Enter team contracts, individual nil results, and books won; Lock It Up calculates the score.
  • Four-player seating. Name each player, seat partners across, optionally swap opponent seats, and rotate the dealer.
  • Clear live scoring. Follow current bids, scores, bags, and round history, with Undo during play.
  • Pause and resume. Keep unfinished games on the device and return to them later.
  • History and stats. Review completed games, team records, win rates, and individual nil performance.
  • Same-platform sync. Confirmed completed-game stats can follow you between iPhones or between Android devices.

Privacy

Local-first. No Widget Corner account. No ads. No cross-app tracking. Rules and games in progress stay on your device. Only confirmed completed-game records are uploaded for same-platform stats sync; platform services may also process account, analytics, and diagnostics data as explained in the privacy policy.
