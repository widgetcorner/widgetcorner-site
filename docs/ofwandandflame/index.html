A closer look

Let your practice find its rhythm.

Of Wand & Flame is a SwiftUI app for witchcraft practitioners — a quiet, on-device companion built around eight sabbat arcs, the moon’s cycle, and your own evolving grimoire. Grounded in Ursula Seren’s voice, it’s meant to sit beside your practice, not get in its way.

The app is private by design: there is no remote backend, no analytics, no advertising. Everything you write stays on your device.

The little details

For each turning of the year.

  • Wheel of the Year hub

    Eight sabbat arcs — history, lore, correspondences, and celebrate prompts to mark each turning of the year.

  • Moon phase tracker

    Current-phase guidance tied to your practice — what each phase invites you to do.

  • Spells library

    Read prebuilt spells, save your favorites, and write your own.

  • Grimoire / Book of Shadows

    A private place for personal spells and notes, kept on your device.

  • Correspondences reference

    Herbs, colors, and intents — a quick lookup for when you’re building a working.

  • About the practitioner

    A section grounding the app in Ursula Seren’s voice and approach.

  • Sign in with Apple

    A single, private credential — no password, no email signup, no third-party login.

Privacy

Your time. Your device. Your business.

No remote backend. No analytics. No third-party SDKs. Your spells, notes, and preferences live on your device in SwiftData and Keychain — and stay there.
