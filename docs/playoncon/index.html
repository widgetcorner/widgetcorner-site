A closer look

Make the most of your weekend.

Play On Con is a four-day tabletop and gaming convention at the Alabama 4-H Center in Columbiana, Alabama, featuring theater, RPGs, board games, video games, parties, and outdoor activities.

Connect once to sync, then take your schedule and venue map offline. Navigate sessions and venues even where cell signal is spotty — cabins, the Lodge, or out on the grounds.

The little details

Everything you need to get around.

  • Full four-day schedule

    Every session across every venue. The view auto-opens to the current time slot.

  • My Schedule

    Save sessions you want to attend.

  • Reminders

    Local notifications at start time or a custom number of minutes ahead. No account, no push server.

  • Venue map with “you are here.”

    Color-coded pins for Stages, Gaming, Parties, Outdoors, and Stay & Eat. Location use is opt-in.

  • Tap to explore

    See what’s happening at each venue. Jump from any session to its venue pin.

  • Session tags

    Ages 21+, PG-13, Apprentice Track, Sensory Friendly, Sign-up at Open Gaming.

  • Discord link and sync status

    Quick community access and a clear last-synced indicator.

Privacy

Your time. Your device. Your business.

No account. No login. No ads. No tracking. Zero data collected. Location use is opt-in and stays on your device.
