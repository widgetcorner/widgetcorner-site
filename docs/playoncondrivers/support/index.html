Support

Help with the Play On Con Drivers app — for the volunteers driving shuttle carts.

Frequently asked

How do I sign in for my shift?

Open the app, type a name or nickname (it does not have to be your real name), and choose the cart you’re driving — Cart 1, Cart 2, or Cart 3. Tap Start broadcasting when you’re ready to begin.

I tapped Start broadcasting but attendees aren’t seeing me.

Make sure you granted location permission and that the device has signal. Check that the broadcasting indicator is on. If you’re still having trouble, stop broadcasting, force-quit the app, reopen, and start broadcasting again.

What permissions does the app need?

Location, while you have broadcasting turned on. iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Play On Con Drivers. Android: Settings → Location → App permissions → Play On Con Drivers.

Does the app track me when I’m not driving?

No. The app only sends location while broadcasting is on. Tap Stop broadcasting, or close the app, and it stops.

Can I use a nickname?

Yes. The sign-in name does not have to be your real name. Pick something other volunteers will recognize on shift.

What if my phone dies or loses signal?

Broadcasting stops. When signal comes back, open the app and tap Start broadcasting again. Past gaps don’t need to be backfilled — attendees only need to see where you are right now.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Email support@widgetcorner.com. Include your device model and OS version if possible.
