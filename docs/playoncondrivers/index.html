Play On Con Drivers

A simple companion for Play On Con shuttle volunteers. Pick your cart and share its live location, so attendees know when a ride is coming.

The app is in development and not yet available on the stores.

  • Pick your cart
  • Start and stop with a tap

↗

A closer look

A helping hand for the people behind the wheel.

Play On Con Drivers is a small purpose-built app for the volunteers who drive the shuttle carts during Play On Con. Sign in for your shift, pick the cart you’re driving, and tap to start broadcasting. While you’re broadcasting, your cart’s position appears in real time inside the main Play On Con app so attendees know when a ride is coming.

When you’re done driving, tap stop. The app does nothing in the background.

The little details

Less to manage. More time to drive.

  • Sign in with a name and cart

    The name can be a nickname — it does not have to be your real name. Pick Cart 1, Cart 2, or Cart 3.

  • One-tap broadcasting

    Tap Start broadcasting when you begin a shift. Tap Stop when you’re done.

  • Live shuttle location

    While broadcasting, the app sends GPS, direction, and speed every ten seconds so attendees can see the cart move.

  • No background tracking

    The app only sends location while broadcasting is on. Stop broadcasting and it stops sending.

  • Built for spotty signal

    The app is designed for the kind of coverage you get on convention grounds, not downtown 5G.

Privacy

Your time. Your device. Your business.

The app only sends data while you have broadcasting turned on. It sends GPS, direction, speed, your selected cart, and the name you typed at sign-in — and nothing else. No contacts, photos, microphone, camera, web history, or advertising IDs are touched.
