BurlyCon

The companion app for BurlyCon — full schedule, venue floor plan, and reminders. Works offline. No account required.

About

BurlyCon is an annual burlesque arts education convention, held November 5–8, 2026 at the Hilton Seattle Airport & Conference Center in Seattle, Washington.

The companion app for iOS and Android includes a bundled schedule and venue floor plan, so you can browse classes and find rooms even without a connection. Connect to refresh the schedule with the latest updates.

Features

  • Convention schedule. Browse classes, panels, and events by day. Check Home for coming-up sessions.
  • Day chips and search. Jump between Thursday–Sunday and search by title, presenter, or room.
  • My Con. Save the sessions you want to attend, grouped by day.
  • Reminders. Choose a local notification for a session’s start time or up to two hours ahead. Notification permission is required; delivery timing depends on your device.
  • Venue floor plan. Zoom in on the venue map to find the room named in your session details. The floor plan is included for offline use.
  • Info and links. Presenter biographies, registration links, hotel details, and accessibility information in one place. External websites require a connection.

Privacy

No account, ads, or analytics. Your saved sessions and reminder settings are stored locally and are not sent to Widget Corner. See the privacy policy for details about content downloads, device backups, and support email.
