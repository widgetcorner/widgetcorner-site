A closer look

A little guidance. A lot to discover.

BurlyCon is an annual burlesque arts education convention, held November 5–8, 2026 at the Hilton Seattle Airport & Conference Center in Seattle, Washington.

The companion app for iPhone, iPad, and Android includes a bundled schedule, venue map, and text room guide, so you can browse classes and look up room locations even without a connection. Connect to refresh the schedule with the latest updates.

The little details

Make room for a great weekend.

  • Convention schedule

    Browse classes, panels, and events by day. Check Home for coming-up sessions.

  • Day chips and search

    Jump between Thursday–Sunday and search by title, presenter, or room.

  • My Con

    Save the sessions you want to attend, grouped by day.

  • Reminders

    Choose a local notification for a session’s start time or up to two hours ahead. Notification permission is required; delivery timing depends on your device.

  • Venue map and room guide

    Read room descriptions or explore the Cascade Building map with zoom, pan, and reset buttons. Both views are included for offline use.

  • Info and links

    Presenter biographies, registration links, hotel details, and accessibility information in one place. External websites require a connection.

More ways to make it yours

A more comfortable convention companion.

Version 2026.9.15 (build 10) adds accessibility improvements across phones and tablets. Features depend on the app version installed on your device.

  • Larger text

    The app follows your device’s text-size setting, with wrapping titles, scrollable dialogs, and navigation that adapts to the available space.

  • Light and dark appearance

    The app follows your device’s theme, with updated text and control contrast. The room guide follows Dark Mode; the map image keeps its light background.

  • Reduced motion

    Navigation, dialogs, and refresh indicators respond to your device’s reduced-motion setting.

  • Clearer controls

    Descriptive headings and button labels help assistive technology identify content and actions. Selected filters and saved sessions use text or symbols as well as color.

See accessibility help for settings, map instructions, and the current status of screen-reader and voice-control testing.
